About us

Straightforward dealing, across borders.

Waste to Energy (UK) Ltd was founded in 2014 and is registered in England and Wales. Over more than a decade, the company has worked with buyers, manufacturers and project partners across the UK, Europe, Africa and the Middle East, sourcing equipment and supporting projects in energy, waste and industry.

The company is led by its founder and director, who brings over ten years of experience connecting international buyers with suppliers and coordinating cross-border projects. Our approach is simple: understand what the client needs, bring in the right specialists, and deal straightforwardly at every stage.
